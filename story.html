Did you know?

Some Pharaohs Were Kids!

King Tutankhamun became Pharaoh when he was only about 9 years old. Imagine being the ruler of Egypt while you were still a child!

Ancient Egypt Was Full of Color!

The temples and statues of Ancient Egypt were once painted with bright, colorful paints. They didn't look plain and sandy like they do today.

They Had Board Games!

One of the most popular games was Senet, a board game enjoyed by both children and adults over 5,000 years ago. It was one of the world's oldest known board games.
