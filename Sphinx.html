Long ago, a young prince named Thutmose was exploring near the Great Sphinx at Giza.


The giant stone Sphinx was almost completely covered by sand. Tired from the hot sun, the prince rested in its shade and soon fell asleep.


According to the ancient story, the Sphinx spoke to him in a dream.


It asked the prince to clear away the sand that covered its body. The Sphinx promised that if he cared for it, he would one day become the ruler of Egypt.


When Thutmose woke up, he remembered the dream. He ordered workers to uncover the Great Sphinx and protect it from the sand.


Years later, Thutmose became Pharaoh Thutmose IV. To remember the special dream, he placed a large stone monument called the Dream Stela between the Sphinx's front paws.


Today, visitors can still see the Dream Stela and the Great Sphinx, which has watched over the Giza Plateau for thousands of years.

Did You Know?

The Great Sphinx is one of the largest stone statues in the world.

Did You Know?

The Dream Stela was placed there by Thutmose IV.

New words

Sphinx

A giant stone statue with the body of a lion and the head of a person.

Dream

The pictures and stories your mind creates while you are sleeping.

Stela

A tall stone monument with words or pictures carved into it.

Prince

The son of a king or queen.

The Dream Stela

The Dream Stela is a large stone monument placed between the paws of the Great Sphinx by Thutmose IV. It tells the famous story of the prince's dream and is still standing at Giza today.
